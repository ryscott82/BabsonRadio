# Booth troubleshooting

Something not working in the studio? Start here. If none of this fixes it, post in the Babson Sound WhatsApp. Never rewire the gear yourself.

## The booth says "No sound"

The studio PC hears nothing from the mixer.

- Check your mic's fader is up and its channel is not muted.
- Check the mixer is on.
- Check the mic cable is plugged in at both ends.
- Talk close to the mic, a few inches away.

## The meter says "Too loud" (red)

The sound is clipping, which distorts for every listener and can't be fixed afterwards. Turn the mixer down until the meter says Good.

## The meter says "Too quiet"

Move closer to the mic and turn the mixer up a little. Turning up your headphones or the speakers does not change what goes out on the stream.

## You hear yourself a few seconds late

You are listening to the live stream (a phone or the website). Turn it off and listen through the mixer's headphone output. The stream always runs a few seconds behind.

## Nothing in your headphones, but the meter says Good

The stream is fine. Check your headphones are plugged into the mixer and its headphone volume is up.

## Go live won't start

Go live needs three things:

- You are signed in.
- The booth has heard you say something into the mic.
- You have ticked the clean music box.

## "The studio helper isn't running"

Wait a few seconds. It restarts by itself. If the message stays, post in the WhatsApp. Don't restart or unplug the PC.

## "BUTT isn't answering"

BUTT is the program that sends the sound to the station. Post in the WhatsApp so the E-board can open it again.

## Not sure about a word or a lyric

Leave it out, then check the [Explicit phrase reference](https://babsonradio.com/words) or ask the E-board.

## Before you leave

Press **End show**, then sign out, and leave the desk and mics tidy. Walking away leaves dead air on the station.
