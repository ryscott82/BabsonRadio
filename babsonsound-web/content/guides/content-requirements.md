# Content requirements

This is what you agree to when you tick the content box on a show request. The full wording is in the agreement you sign in the training.

## Clean hours: 6 AM to 8 PM

- Play no explicit songs between 6 AM and 8 PM.
- Check any phrase you are unsure of in the [Explicit phrase reference](/words) before you air it.
- After 8 PM you still keep foul language to a minimum.

## Slurs and egregious words

- Never air an egregious word or a slur, at any hour.
- If one slips out, apologize on air once and move on. Do not repeat the word.
- A slur is any word or name that demeans a person or group for a protected trait. It counts in any language, in songs, clips and quotes, when it is bleeped or hinted at, and even when the speaker belongs to that group.

## Private information

Never share anyone's private information on air, including a phone number, email or home address.

## You are responsible for your show

- You are responsible for everything on air during your time. Staff decide what airs and can change or cancel a slot.
- Add every song you play (title and artist) to your set list during or right after each show, and before it airs for a pre-recorded show. The station reports these to the rights holders. Missing lists can cost you future slots.
- A pre-recorded show must be clean. Listen to the whole recording before you submit it.

## Recordings

Sessions and shows in the studio are recorded. Recordings are for Babson students only, unless you are 18 or older and sign a separate agreement to make one public.
