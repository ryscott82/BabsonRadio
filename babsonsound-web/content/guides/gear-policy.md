# Gear policy

The studio gear belongs to Babson Sound. This is what you agree to when you tick the gear box on a show request. The full wording is in the agreement you sign in the training.

## Using the gear

- Only trained members may use the Radio Room gear. Never bring an untrained member into the room.
- Sign in with the door QR code or your Babson Sound account before you touch any equipment. Scan the door QR code before tapping your OneCard.
- Use the gear the way you were trained.

## What counts as misuse

- Rewiring, unplugging or moving gear that is part of the setup.
- Taking anything out of the Radio Room.
- Using the gear without signing in, or letting someone else use it on your sign-in.
- Leaving mics on, or leaving the desk messy.

## When something breaks

Report it in the Babson Sound WhatsApp right away, with what happened. Reporting a fault quickly is never a strike. Hiding it can be.

## Bookings and leaving

- A booking reserves the studio. Leave on time and respect other people's bookings.
- Keep the volume as low as possible during quiet hours.
- When you leave, the studio is as you found it: mics off, desk clear, gear back where it was, and you are signed out of the booth.

## What happens if the rules are broken

Breaking the gear rules can mean losing studio access, your slots and your recordings, and a report to Babson. Missed shows and bookings are strikes: the first sends a warning email, the second pauses studio bookings for a week, and the third bans hosting and booking for the semester.
