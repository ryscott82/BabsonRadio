Members Benefits

Join a collaborative network of DJs, live musicians, producers, and music enthusiasts. Members receive hands-on training and access to our fully equipped recording studio (the Radio Room in the basement of PM Central) for their own passion projects and recorded media. Beyond the studio, Babson Sound provides direct opportunities to perform at active campus events, giving student artists the platform to showcase their talent and gain live entertainment experience.
