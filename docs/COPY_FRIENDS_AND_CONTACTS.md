# Friends and contacts wording

Every message a person sees about contacts, friends and Invisible mode. Replace anything under "Your wording" and send the file back (or just paste the new text into chat). Anything left blank keeps the current text. Names are always first names only.

Rules the wording has to stay true to (they are how the code works):
- Names appear only for someone who already has you in their contacts, is connected with you on the app, and is tuned in.
- Invisible mode: hides you from friends, hides "your friend liked this", turns live reactions off (sending and seeing), and stops contact suggestions. It does not stop station analytics.
- Contacts: school emails and the word Babson are read on the phone. Phone numbers are scrambled on the phone before they are sent. We keep scrambled codes only, never names. A number is suggested only when 3+ other members saved it, and nobody is told who saved whom.
